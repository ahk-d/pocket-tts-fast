pocket-tts-fast · in your browser

Same model. Same audio. Faster.

This page runs Kyutai Pocket TTS on your device twice: once with the stock 1,000-slot KV cache, once with the cache sized to the sentence. Nothing is sent to a server.

    First run downloads about 125 MB of model files, then they are cached.
