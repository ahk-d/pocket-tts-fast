What these numbers mean
  • Ready: model loaded once. Voice changes reuse it.
  • Starts speaking: time from Generate to scheduled playback.
  • Generation: time to finish the complete recording.
  • Playback gaps: waiting for more generated audio, excluding natural pauses.
  • RTF below 1: audio is generated faster than it plays.
